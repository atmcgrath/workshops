# Activity

- What do you want AI to do for you? 
- In pairs: brainstorm 2-3 different prompts and write them down in a document.
- Consider these categories: technical help, task help, fun/experimentation

## Prompt engineering {.smaller}

- Navigate to [Microsoft Copilot](https://copilot.microsoft.com/).
  - Select 'work' and log in with your BMC credentials
- With your partner, test out the prompts you came up with
  - Be specific and detailed
  - Refine your prompt with follow-up questions
  - Test any code outputs to see if it works
- Save the output in the same document

## Discussion

Share your prompts and results with the class.

- How did Copilot do? 
- What did you learn from the process?
